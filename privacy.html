Privacy Policy

This app is a private tool used only by its owner and invited family members.

  • It accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data only for the account holder who signs in, to read, label and organize that person's own mail and calendar.
  • Data is processed on a private home server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data is collected from anyone who has not signed in.
  • Any user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
